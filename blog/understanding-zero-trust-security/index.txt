10:Ta31,<p>Zero Trust is built on a simple but powerful principle: <strong>never trust, always verify</strong>. Instead of assuming everything inside a network perimeter is safe, Zero Trust requires continuous verification of every user, device, and connection.</p>
<h2>What Is Zero Trust?</h2>
<p>Traditional security models operated on a "castle and moat" paradigm — once you were inside the perimeter, you were trusted. Zero Trust flips this model entirely. Every request for access is treated as if it originates from an untrusted network, whether it comes from inside or outside the organisation.</p>
<p>The three core tenets of Zero Trust are:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Verify explicitly</strong> — Always authenticate and authorise using all available data points</li>
<li><strong>Use least privilege access</strong> — Limit user access with just-in-time and just-enough-access policies</li>
<li><strong>Assume breach</strong> — Minimise blast radius, segment access, and verify end-to-end encryption</li>
</ol>
<h2>Why Zero Trust Matters in 2026</h2>
<p>The traditional network perimeter has dissolved. Remote work, cloud services, and mobile devices have made the concept of a trusted "inside" network obsolete. Attackers increasingly use compromised credentials and lateral movement to reach valuable assets.</p>
<p>Key drivers of Zero Trust adoption:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>85% of breaches</strong> involve the human element, including social attacks, errors, and misuse</li>
<li>The average time to identify a breach is still measured in months</li>
<li>Cloud-based attacks have increased significantly year on year</li>
</ul>
<h2>Implementing Zero Trust at Skillfield</h2>
<p>At Skillfield, we help organisations implement Zero Trust architectures that are practical and phased. Key components include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Identity and Access Management (IAM)</strong> — Centralised identity with multi-factor authentication</li>
<li><strong>Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)</strong> — Continuous monitoring and verification of device health</li>
<li><strong>Micro-segmentation</strong> — Dividing the network into small zones to contain breaches</li>
<li><strong>Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)</strong> — Centralised visibility across all events</li>
</ul>
<h2>Getting Started</h2>
<p>Zero Trust is a journey, not a destination. The key is to start with your most critical assets and highest-risk areas, then expand coverage incrementally.</p>
