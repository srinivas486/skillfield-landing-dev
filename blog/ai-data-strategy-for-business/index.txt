10:T983,<p>Artificial intelligence promises transformative business outcomes — but without the right data foundation, AI projects often fail to deliver. Here's how to build a strategy that works.</p>
<h2>The Data-First Imperative</h2>
<p>Before any AI initiative can succeed, organisations need high-quality, accessible data. The most sophisticated machine learning model is only as good as the data it's trained on.</p>
<p>Common data challenges we encounter:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Data silos</strong> — Critical information locked in disconnected systems</li>
<li><strong>Data quality</strong> — Inconsistent, incomplete, or outdated records</li>
<li><strong>Governance gaps</strong> — No clear ownership or lifecycle management</li>
<li><strong>Infrastructure constraints</strong> — On-premises systems that limit scalability</li>
</ul>
<h2>Building Your Data Platform</h2>
<p>A modern data platform typically consists of:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Data ingestion layer</strong> — ETL/ELT pipelines that reliably move data from source systems</li>
<li><strong>Storage and processing</strong> — Cloud data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks)</li>
<li><strong>Data governance</strong> — Catalogues, lineage tracking, and access controls</li>
<li><strong>Analytics and AI layer</strong> — BI tools, feature stores, and model serving infrastructure</li>
</ol>
<h2>From Data to AI</h2>
<p>Once the data foundation is solid, AI initiatives have a much higher chance of success. Practical applications Skillfield has delivered include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Predictive maintenance</strong> for manufacturing and utilities clients</li>
<li><strong>Customer churn prediction</strong> for subscription-based businesses</li>
<li><strong>Automated anomaly detection</strong> for financial services</li>
<li><strong>Natural language processing</strong> for document processing and compliance</li>
</ul>
<h2>Measuring Success</h2>
<p>AI and data projects need clear metrics from the start. We help clients define:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Business KPIs</strong> — Revenue impact, cost reduction, efficiency gains</li>
<li><strong>Technical metrics</strong> — Model accuracy, latency, data freshness</li>
<li><strong>Operational metrics</strong> — Pipeline reliability, data quality scores</li>
</ul>
